# Announcement — paste into Workspace → Announcements

**Headline:** Meet now calls now ring on your laptop and phone — set it up today

**Attach:** `Focus-Realm-Meet-Now-and-Phone-Notifications-Guide.pdf` · **Pin to the top:** yes

---

Hi everyone,

From today, **Meet now** works like a phone call. When someone calls you from the Workspace (Calendar → Meet now, or Meet now on their profile):

• **On your laptop** — if a Workspace tab is open, a full-screen card pops up ("… is inviting you to a call right now") with a **ringtone**. Click **Join** to go straight into Google Meet, or **Not now**.
• **On your phone** — you get a call notification that **rings and vibrates every 15 seconds for 90 seconds**, with **Join** / **Not now**. If you miss it, it turns into a "Missed call" notification.
• Answer on one device and the others stop ringing.

Your phone will also notify you about new tasks, meeting invites and changes, announcements and deadline reminders.

**Please set this up today (about 5 minutes) — the attached guide has step-by-step instructions:**

1. **Laptop:** open the Workspace in Chrome/Edge → on Home tap **Turn on** under "Don't miss a call" → **Allow**. Keep one Workspace tab open during work hours so the ringtone can play.
2. **Android:** open the Workspace in Chrome → ⋮ → **Add to Home screen / Install app** → open **Focus Realm** from your home screen → **Turn on** → **Allow**.
3. **iPhone (iOS 16.4+):** open the Workspace in **Safari** → Share → **Add to Home Screen** → open **Focus Realm** from your home screen, sign in → **Turn on** → **Allow**.
4. Check it works: **Settings → Notifications → Send a test**.

Tick **"Keep me signed in on this device"** when you sign in on your own phone and laptop — signing out stops notifications on that device.

If anything doesn't work, see section 6 of the guide or message a founder.

# Second announcement (post once "My drive" is live)

**Headline:** Your work now has a permanent home — My drive

Everything you hand in or add to a task — documents, links and written hand-ins — now collects in **My drive** (left menu, or **Open drive** on your profile). It stays there after the work is accepted and the task is done, so nothing gets lost. Founders (and anyone cleared to see your work) can open it from your profile.
